Kalisi

Your mail and your work, on your own computer.

Mac

Macs from 2015 onward

Download for Mac
  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. Double-click Kalisi. It installs itself in Applications.
  3. Kalisi opens in your browser.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11

Download for Windows
  1. Open the downloaded file and choose Extract all.
  2. Open Kalisi.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose Yes when Windows asks to install the Kalisi certificate.
  4. Kalisi opens in your browser.
Every download is on GitHub.
